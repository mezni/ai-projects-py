# Business Internet 1000

## Overview

Business Internet 1000 is a fictional business broadband
service with a maximum advertised download speed of 1000 Mbps.

## Service prerequisites

- Confirm service availability at the business address.
- Verify the installed access technology.
- Confirm the customer equipment supports the service.
- Verify that the order has completed provisioning.

## Support requirements

When a business customer reports an outage, collect the
affected service identifier, outage start time, and relevant
gateway or network alarm information.

Use the approved troubleshooting runbook before escalating.
