# Fiber 500 Service

## Overview

Fiber 500 is a fictional residential broadband service
with a maximum advertised download speed of 500 Mbps.

## Installation prerequisites

- Fiber service must be available at the service address.
- An optical network terminal (ONT) must be installed.
- The customer gateway must support the service profile.
- The installation appointment must be confirmed.

## Troubleshooting guidance

If the service is unavailable after activation:

1. Confirm the ONT has power.
2. Inspect the optical status indicator.
3. Check the gateway's WAN connection.
4. Consult the optical signal loss runbook if the ONT
   reports a loss-of-signal condition.

## Limitations

Advertised speeds are maximum service rates, not a
guarantee of throughput under every network condition.
