# Optical Signal Loss Troubleshooting

## Symptom

The optical network terminal reports a loss-of-signal
condition, or an associated network alarm is raised.

## Possible causes

- Fiber disconnection or damage.
- Optical distribution network fault.
- Remote network equipment issue.
- Optical signal outside the supported operating range.

## Diagnostic procedure

1. Verify the alarm identifier and timestamp.
2. Check the ONT power and optical indicators.
3. Inspect accessible customer-side connections only
   according to approved procedures.
4. Check for related alarms and known outages.
5. Escalate suspected outside-plant or network faults
   to the responsible operations team.

## Safety and escalation

Do not open network equipment or perform optical
maintenance unless authorized and appropriately trained.

Record observed symptoms and diagnostic evidence.
Do not declare the fault resolved until service
restoration has been confirmed.
