# Business Internet Service SLA

This fictional policy applies to the Business Internet 1000
service when the service contract explicitly references it.

This target is synthetic and does not establish an actual
contractual entitlement.

## Incident handling

- Incident response targets must be taken from the applicable
  contract and support schedule.
- Response time is not the same as resolution time.
- Service restoration must be confirmed before closing an incident.

## Exclusions and verification

Do not assume that a maintenance window, customer-side fault,
or third-party issue is excluded unless the governing contract
explicitly states the applicable exclusion.

Before answering a customer-specific SLA question, verify
the contract version, service scope, and applicable terms.
